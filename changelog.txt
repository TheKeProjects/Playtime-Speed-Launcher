4.1.0
## Additions
- Leaderboards: a run's YouTube video now plays inside the launcher, like the skips and glitches tutorials (other sites open in the browser)
- Leaderboards: click a runner to see the run's date, country, comment and links to the run and their profile
- New hand mod preview: one big picture per hand, browse with the arrows or the arrow keys, and install it right there
- The checkpoint menu can be used with the keyboard (arrows to pick, Enter to load), a double click loads a checkpoint, and it confirms when something was loaded or deleted
- Downloading SteamCMD shows each step and a progress bar with the downloaded size, and can be cancelled
- The beginner tutorials remember which videos you've watched and have a Next button to go to the following one

## Redesigns
- Leaderboards: chapter and category tabs, a podium for the top 3 and the full top 10 with each runner's flag, name colour, gap to the record and how long ago the run was
- Hand Mods: chapter and version cards, a step bar, screens sliding in and out, loading placeholders, a retry button and a new install screen with a progress ring
- Hand Mods: the Individual Hands menu is now a grid of picture cards, and the Hand Conflict warning shows what goes in and which mods will be removed first
- Checkpoint menu (hotkey): new look, with animations when it opens and closes and checkpoints sliding in one by one
- Beginner tutorials: a 4-step path, cards that explain each video and a step list beside the video while watching
- SteamCMD install window: explains what SteamCMD is and both ways to get it

## Seasonal Themes
- The checkpoint, skips and glitches, skip report, leaderboards, beginner tutorials and Hand Mods menus now follow the seasonal themes (Halloween, Summer and Christmas)
- Those menus get the season's decorations on their top edge and their own particles: snow, embers with a bat or bubbles and glints

## Performance
- Menus that play videos no longer redraw at your monitor's full refresh rate while a video is loaded
- The new menus only keep redrawing while something is actually moving, and their decorative animations settle down after a few seconds
- The Hand Mods menu no longer checks the mod files on disk every frame, and hand pictures load in the background

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed a tutorial video staying on screen after going back to the list
- Fixed the SteamCMD download status always showing in Spanish
