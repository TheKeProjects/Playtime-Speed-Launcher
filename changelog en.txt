4.3.1
## Additions
Duels: click any player to open their mini profile, and from there their full profile
The full profile now shows the player's profile frame
Friends: new All Users page with every launcher account, online and offline
Chat: talk with the players in your duel lobby, and send private messages to your friends
Duels: the ranking is now monthly, and each month's winner gets a champion title everyone can see
Duels: forfeiting 2 duels in a row blocks forfeiting for 1 hour
Badges: champion, pioneer, contributor and developer badges, all shown in the profile's Badges tab; pick the one next to your name in the editor
Profile editor: a Preview button to see your profile as others see it
New GrabPack 67 avatar decoration
Duel notifications: more options, like your turn to ban, a rival finishing or friend invites only

## Bug Fixes
Texts and labels over nameplates are now readable in the rankings, friends and duel lists
