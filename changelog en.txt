4.3.0
## Additions
Launcher accounts: your playtime is saved in the cloud, so you keep your hours on any PC
Profiles: name, picture, banner and cosmetics, plus your linked speedrun.com and Discord accounts
Community ranking: launcher players ranked by their speedrun.com personal bests on each chapter
Friends: add people by name or friend code and see who's online and what they're playing
Duels: race other launcher players in 1v1 up to 6-player rooms and climb the MMR ranks

## Bug Fixes
Locked chapters no longer show playtime, multiplayer games show theirs even when not installed, and times under a minute show in seconds
