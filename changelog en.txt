4.1.4
## Additions
Escape From Playtime is out: the launcher finds your Steam install and launches it
New Updates button next to Settings, with the Updates and Changelog pages side by side (it glows red when there's a new version)
Add to Steam is now a button next to the quit button, with a window that shows each step live
Installing or removing Load Manip and FullBright shows an animated loading screen and the result in the same popup
## Redesigns
New look for the bug and skip reports, with a checklist that ticks itself as you fill them in
New look for the mod popups, saves and checkpoints, LiveSplit, manual update, tutorials and notifications
Settings: sliding ON / OFF switches and animated tabs and options
## Seasonal Themes
Every menu and window now has the season's decorations and particles
## Bug Fixes
Fixed runners' pictures not showing in the leaderboards for many people
